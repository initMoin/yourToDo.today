A focused task system

What do you want toDō?

Intention precedes everything. toDō exists to carry that intention forward through structure, clarity, and momentum. Designed to stay out of your way while remaining powerful when needed, it provides a dependable system for managing responsibilities, pursuing goals, and moving from intention to completion. Along the way, it makes progress visible, keeps unfinished work actionable, and makes accountability a natural part of the process.

intention

Built around intention.

toDō was borne of a need to remember, to track, to accomplish, all in pursuit of success.

Every task begins with intention. The challenge isn't remembering what needs to be done—it's preserving that intention long enough to turn it into meaningful action. toDō was designed to reduce the friction between deciding and doing.

Rather than overwhelming people with complexity or stripping away useful context, it aims to provide structure where structure helps, while remaining unobtrusive when it doesn't. The system should support your work, not become additional work itself.

Every decision throughout the app follows that same objective: keep the interface clear, the workflow deliberate, and the path from intention to completion as direct as possible. Those principles don't exist only as ideas; they shape every interaction, every feature, and every part of the system that follows.

Direction

toDō has been shaped by years of observations, ideas, and lived experience. Version 3 represents the strongest expression of that work so far, bringing together lessons learned from earlier releases, evolving platform capabilities, and a clearer understanding of what a task management system should be.

The goal remains unchanged. Build something useful, dependable, and intentional. Every future release should expand the system without compromising the clarity, structure, and momentum that give toDō its character.

source

Built from necessity. Continued with intention.

Independently engineered through more than seven years of continuous iteration, refinement, and long-term commitment.

toDō began as a personal system during recovery, when memory and cognitive function became distinctly unreliable. What started as a way to regain structure eventually became an ongoing effort to build a task management system centered on intention, clarity, and momentum.

More than seven years later, that same commitment continues to guide every release. New features are added with care, existing ideas are continually refined, and every decision is measured against the same intention that started the project. Progress isn't defined by how much changes, but by whether each change genuinely improves the system.
