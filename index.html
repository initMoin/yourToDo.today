Intention precedes everything. toDō exists to carry that intention forward through structure, clarity, and momentum. about

Built to reduce drag.

toDō was borne of a need to remember, to track, to accomplish, all in pursuit of success.

Most task apps either bury you in features or flatten everything into the same lifeless list. toDō aims for the middle path: structured enough to support real life, simple enough to stay out of your way.

A task can carry context, notes, timing, recurrence, and organization without becoming a ritual. The point is not to make productivity louder. The point is to make action easier.

It is a product built around attention, pacing, and usability. The interface is meant to feel clear, direct, and native rather than busy, decorative, or over-explained.

Why it exists

toDō began as a personal system during recovery, when memory and cognitive function became distinctly unreliable. What started as a way to regain structure eventually became a broader effort to build a task system centered on intention, clarity, and momentum.

direction

A product with room to grow.

toDō has been shaped by years of observations, ideas, and lived experience. Version 3.0 represents the strongest expression of that work so far, bringing together lessons learned from earlier releases, evolving platform capabilities, and a clearer understanding of what a task system should be.

The goal remains unchanged: build something useful, dependable, and intentional. Future growth should expand the system without compromising the clarity that gives it value.
